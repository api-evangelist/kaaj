# Kaaj

> Kaaj is the underwriting OS for SMB lending. The agentic AI credit intelligence platform helps small business lenders, brokers, equipment finance companies, MCA funders, and community banks turn messy borrower packages into decision-ready credit analysis in under five minutes - across document intake, KYB business verification, bank statement analysis, fraud signal detection, financial spreading, and credit memo generation. Every output is evidence-backed, source-linked, and human-reviewable by design.
>
> Founded in San Francisco by Utsav Shah (CEO, ex-Uber, ex-Cruise) and Shivi Sharma (President, ex-American Express, ex-Varo Bank, ex-Uber). Backed by a $3.8M seed round led by Kindred Ventures with participation from Better Tomorrow Ventures, Karman Ventures, Pythia Ventures, and Coughdrop Capital. SOC 2 Type II compliant. Over $15B in loan originations processed across customers including Amur Equipment Finance, Quality Equipment Finance, Smarter Finance USA, Fundr, Brickhouse Capital, Fortis Equipment Funding, I90 Financial, and others.

## Product capabilities

- [Document Intelligence](https://kaaj.ai/document-intelligence): Ingests deal packages from email, portal, drive, or API and automatically classifies, renames, and organizes every file across 40+ document types. Detects missing required documents, flags duplicates and resubmissions, and extracts key fields (legal name, EIN, dates, amounts) from PDFs, images, spreadsheets, and handwritten or scanned files.
- [KYB & Business Verification](https://kaaj.ai/kyb-business-verification): Consolidates Secretary of State filings, web presence and domain analysis, Google Maps and Street View address verification, ownership and principal name matching, business directory listings, and formation-date checks into one verification profile. Cross-verifies application data against driver's license, voided check, bank statements, tax returns, and equipment invoices.
- [Bank Statement Analysis](https://kaaj.ai/bank-statement-analysis): Goes beyond OCR with industry-aware classification of revenue, transfers, MCA proceeds, owner draws, and loan payments. Tracks NSFs, overdrafts, negative balance days, average daily balance trends, and MCA stacking exposure. Applies industry context: the same Zelle deposit may be revenue for a trucking company but flagged for a restaurant.
- [Credit Memo Automation](https://kaaj.ai/credit-memo-automation): Generates structured credit memos with executive summary, KYB verification results, cashflow analysis, fraud and risk signals, and policy exceptions linked to past precedent. Every claim in the memo is traceable to a source document, agent observation, or verification result. Human-in-the-loop by design: no black box decisions.
- [Fraud Detection](https://kaaj.ai/fraud-detection): Surfaces document tampering, PDF metadata anomalies, name and address mismatches across application/SOS/bank/DL, duplicate EIN submissions, suspicious web presence, invoice/application inconsistencies, and cross-application pattern signals at intake before credit analysts spend time on a deal.
- [Integrations](https://kaaj.ai/integrations): Connects with Salesforce, LOS platforms, CRMs, email inboxes, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, SharePoint, REST API, webhooks, and SSO/SAML. Designed to layer onto existing underwriting operations in days, not months. No rip-and-replace.
